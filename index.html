The Darkest Venture

Back to Collection

Summary

The Darkest Venture an immersive horror adventure text-based game that incorporates branching narratives and emphasizes player agency, allowing users to make impactful choices that shape the storyline and enhance their interactive storytelling experience. The game is inspired by the story of a famous Chinese horror novel series "Tomb Raider Notes," which tells the scary, adventurous tale of the heirs of an ancient family who have been raiding tombs for generations to explore ancient tombs. The game's story is a simplified adaptation of the novel's setting. Players would play as a young man named Alex on a family mission to explore a mysterious tomb, learn its secrets, and even uncover the hidden conspiracy lurking there.

Role

Game Level Designer, Lead Writer

Engine

Twine
